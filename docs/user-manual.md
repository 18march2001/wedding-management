## Getting Started

### How to Log In

1. Open your browser and go to the admin panel URL (e.g., `https://yourstudio.com/admin`).
2. You will see the **Login** screen.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
4. Click **Login**.

> If you forget your password, contact your website administrator to reset it.

### How to Log Out

Click your profile icon or name in the **top-right corner** of the screen and select **Logout**. This securely ends your session.

### Navigating the Panel

The admin panel has a **sidebar menu** on the left. Click any menu item to jump to that section. The current page is highlighted in the sidebar.

On smaller screens, the sidebar may be hidden — tap the menu icon (☰) in the top bar to open it.

## Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after logging in. It gives you a quick overview of your studio's content — total galleries, photos, videos, team members, testimonials, and recent inquiries.

Use the Dashboard to get a quick summary before diving into a specific section.

## Gallery Management

Galleries are the core of your portfolio. Each gallery groups related photos together (e.g., a specific wedding event or shoot).

### Viewing Galleries

Go to **Galleries** in the sidebar. You will see a list of all your galleries with their name, category, and cover image.

- Use the **Search** box to find a gallery by name.
- Use the **Category filter** to show only galleries in a particular category.

### Creating a New Gallery

1. Click the **+ New Gallery** (or **Add Gallery**) button.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — The gallery name (e.g., "Sharma Wedding — June 2026").
   - **Category** — Select the appropriate category (Wedding, Pre-Wedding, etc.).
   - **Description** — A short description shown on the website.
   - **Cover Image** — Upload a single image that represents the gallery on the website.
3. Click **Save**.

The gallery will now appear in your list and be visible on the website.

### Editing a Gallery

1. Find the gallery in the list.
2. Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon.
3. Update any details.
4. Click **Save**.

### Deleting a Gallery

1. Find the gallery in the list.
2. Click the **Delete** (trash) icon.
3. Confirm the deletion in the popup.

> **Warning:** Deleting a gallery will also remove all photos inside it. This cannot be undone.

## Photo Management

The Photos section lets you upload and manage individual photos inside your galleries.

### Viewing Photos for a Gallery

1. Go to **Photos** in the sidebar.
2. Select a gallery from the dropdown to see its photos.

### Uploading Photos

1. Select the gallery you want to add photos to.
2. Click **Upload Photos** (or drag-and-drop images into the upload area).
3. You can select **multiple photos at once**.
4. Supported formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP (max 10 MB per photo).
5. Wait for the upload to complete — a progress indicator will show.

### Updating a Photo

You can update the **alt text** (a short description of the image, useful for accessibility and SEO):

1. Click the **Edit** icon on any photo.
2. Type a description in the **Alt Text** field.
3. Click **Save**.

### Deleting a Photo

1. Click the **Delete** (trash) icon on the photo you want to remove.
2. Confirm in the popup.

## Video Management

The Videos section manages video content shown on your website (e.g., wedding highlight reels, behind-the-scenes clips).

### Viewing Videos

Go to **Videos** in the sidebar to see all video entries.

### Adding a New Video

1. Click **+ Add Video**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — Name of the video.
   - **URL** — The video link (YouTube, Vimeo, or direct link).
   - **Description** — Optional short description.
3. Click **Save**.

### Editing or Deleting a Video

Use the **Edit** or **Delete** icons next to each video entry, just like in the Galleries section.

## Categories

Categories help organise your galleries into types (e.g., Wedding, Pre-Wedding, Maternity, Product, Interior, Model).

### Viewing Categories

Go to **Categories** in the sidebar.

### Adding a Category

1. Click **+ New Category**.
2. Enter the **Category Name**.
3. Click **Save**.

### Editing or Deleting a Category

- Click **Edit** to rename a category.
- Click **Delete** to remove it.

> **Note:** Deleting a category does not delete the galleries inside it, but those galleries will no longer be associated with a category. Reassign them if needed.

## Testimonials

Testimonials are client reviews displayed on your website to build trust with potential customers.

### Adding a Testimonial

1. Go to **Testimonials** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Testimonial**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Client Name**
   - **Review** — The client's feedback in their own words.
   - **Rating** — A star rating (if applicable).
4. Click **Save**.

### Editing or Deleting a Testimonial

Use the **Edit** and **Delete** icons next to each testimonial.

---

## Team Members

The Team Members section manages the profiles of your photographers and staff shown on the website.

### Adding a Team Member

1. Go to **Team Members** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Member**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**
   - **Role** — (e.g., Lead Photographer, Second Shooter, Editor)
   - **Bio** — A short description about the person.
   - **Photo** — Upload a profile photo.
4. Click **Save**.

### Editing or Deleting a Team Member

Use the **Edit** and **Delete** icons next to each member's entry.

---

## Inquiries

Inquiries are messages sent by visitors through the **Contact form** on your public website.

### Viewing Inquiries

1. Go to **Inquiries** in the sidebar.
2. You will see a list of all messages with the sender's name, email, phone, and date.
3. Click any inquiry to read the full message.

### Deleting an Inquiry

Once you have responded to or resolved an inquiry, you can delete it by clicking the **Delete** icon.

> **Tip:** Reply to inquiries directly via your email client using the email address shown in the inquiry. The admin panel does not send emails — it only stores the messages.

## Website Settings

The Website Settings page controls the hero (banner) section at the top of your public homepage.

### Updating Website Content / Copy

1. Go to **Settings → Website Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Edit your **tagline**, **about text**, or other homepage copy fields.
3. Click **Save**.

### Managing Hero Slider Images

The hero section at the top of your website shows a rotating slideshow of images.

**To add a slider image:**
1. Scroll to the **Slider Images** section.
2. Click **Upload Image** and select your photo.
3. The image will be added to the slideshow automatically.

**To remove a slider image:**
1. Find the image in the slider images list.
2. Click the **Delete** (trash) icon next to it and confirm.

> **Tip:** Use high-quality landscape photos (ideally 1920×1080 or wider) for the best appearance on the hero slider.

## Tips & Best Practices

**Image quality:** Always upload high-resolution images. The system automatically creates optimised versions (thumbnail and medium), but the original quality matters for the full-size view.

**Gallery organisation:** Create categories before creating galleries. This makes it much easier to keep your portfolio organised as it grows.

**Cover images:** Choose a striking, well-composed image as each gallery's cover — this is the first thing visitors see when browsing your portfolio.

**Testimonials:** Add testimonials regularly after completing shoots. Real, recent reviews are one of the most powerful ways to attract new clients.

**Inquiries:** Check your Inquiries section daily so you don't miss a potential booking. Respond promptly — clients often contact multiple photographers at once.

**Slider images:** Keep 4–6 high-impact hero images. Too many can slow down the page; too few makes the slider feel static.

**Logging out:** Always log out when you are done, especially if you are using a shared or public computer.
